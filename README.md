# Pizza-Store-Sales-Analysis  

## Overview  
  -  The dataset consists of 4 related tables: orders, order details, pizzas, and pizza types, which together provide complete information about pizza sales transactions.The dataset is useful for analyzing stability in price, sales trends, revenue performance, and product popularity, helping businesses make data-driven decisions.
  -  The project demonstrates how SQL can be applied in real-world business scenarios to know about their customers and the demand of the products in the market.

## Dataset Description  
  -  It is a relational dataset made up of 4 connected tables:  
    (i) orders – contains order ID, date, and time of each order.  
    (ii) order_details – contains order quantity and pizza details for each order.  
    (iii) pizzas – contains pizza size and price information.  
    (iv) pizza_types – contains pizza name, category, and ingredients.
  -   It includes important business attributes such as order date, order time, pizza type, category, size, quantity, and price.
  -   The dataset contains multiple pizza categories such as Classic, Chicken, Supreme, and Veggie.
  -   Date and time columns allow analysis of daily trends and hourly order distribution.

## Tools & Features used
  -  #### SQL (Structured Query Language)
  -  Aggregate Functions  :
    Functions like SUM(), COUNT(), AVG() were used to calculate total revenue, total orders, and average sales.
  -  GROUP BY Clause  : Used to group data by category, pizza type, date, and hour for analysis.
  -  Join Operations  : Used to combine data from multiple tables (orders, order_details, pizzas, pizza_types) for complete analysis.
  -  Microsoft Word Documentation  : Used to present SQL queries, screenshots of outputs, and project explanations in a structured report format.

## Key Insights and Findings  
  -  Customer are likely to buy more pizzas in October and November months.
  -  The most of the customers prefer to ordered the classic pizza category as compared to others.
  -  There is less demand for the pizza at the opening and closing hours of the business.
  -  The pizza business generated approximately $817,860 in total revenue, indicating healthy business performance.

## Conclusion  
The pizza sales analysis shows that the business performs strongly with high order volume and strong revenue generation. Customer demand is highest for Classic pizzas and Large-sized orders, while a few premium pizzas contribute significantly to revenue. By focusing on popular products, optimizing low-performing items, improving inventory planning, and targeting peak-hour sales, the business can improve profitability and customer satisfaction.
